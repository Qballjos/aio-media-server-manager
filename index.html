    FROM INSTALL TO EVERYDAY USE

    Easy to start. Room to grow.

    1. 1

      Install on your server

      Choose Docker, Unraid, Synology or TrueNAS below. Follow the guide for storage, permissions and starting AIO.

    2. 2

      Choose your apps

      Create your administrator account and follow the setup wizard. Pick the media server and tools you need; add more later.

    3. 3

      Let AIO connect them

      AIO configures supported connections, download clients and library folders. Your provider details and media sources remain yours to supply.

    4. 4

      Make it your own

      Open your apps from one dashboard. Arrange widgets and configure backups, updates and advanced options when you need them.

    HOME, YOUR WAY

    Make room for what matters.

    Your Home screen is yours to arrange. Try the controls in the preview above, then do the same in AIO.

    Try the widget demo ↑
    1. 1

      Open the layout editor

      On Home, choose the four-block layout icon next to the page tabs. Widget handles and an editing toolbar appear.

    2. 2

      Move things around

      Drag a widget to its new place. On a phone, drag its grip so you can still scroll the page.

    3. 3

      Find the right size

      Drag the lower-right corner, or open a widget’s settings and choose a size. Remove a widget there too.

    4. 4

      Add it. Make it yours.

      Choose Add widget, pick This size or Every size, then choose a widget. Changes save automatically; choose Done when finished.

    Phone, tablet and desktop can each have their own layout. Empty widgets may disappear outside edit mode until their connected app has something to show.

    FAQ

    A few things worth knowing.

    Do I need every app?

    No. Start with a media server and the tools you actually need. Add more later from Catalog.

    Where do my movies and shows go?

    They stay on your server. SSD download staging can speed up downloading and unpacking; the main library can stay on your HDDs.

    Do I need a VPN or public domain?

    Neither is required to start on your home network. Both can be configured later.

    Is this a working server in my browser?

    No. The dashboard is a visual demo with sample data. Catalog and Settings show real AIO screenshots. Nothing here connects to your server.
